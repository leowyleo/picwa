Getting started

Click “Add Folder” and choose a folder that contains your images. Picrow scans only folders you explicitly authorize and builds a timeline from the images’ original creation dates.

Images are not appearing

  • Choose a folder containing images, rather than an individual file.
  • Confirm that the image format is supported by macOS ImageIO.
  • Wait for indexing to finish after adding a large folder.

Privacy and file safety

Your original images stay on your Mac. Picrow does not upload images or delete files automatically. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Contact

Report reproducible problems or privacy requests through Picrow Issues on GitHub or leowy.lwy@gmail.com.
